Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address and payment method (handled by Stripe — we never see card numbers).
  • Your Discord user ID, Discord server (guild) ID, and the channel ID you select.
  • Your public Lichess username and the games you play after installation. Game data is fetched from the public Lichess API.
  • Coaching outputs generated for your games (text + images).

What we don't do

  • We don't read messages in your Discord server. The bot uses slash commands only and does not request the Message Content Intent.
  • We don't sell or rent your data to third parties.
  • We don't post anything outside the channel you configured.

Sub-processors

  • Stripe — payments.
  • Discord — bot platform.
  • Lichess — game data source (public API).
  • Groq — LLM inference (game data is sent for analysis).
  • chess-api.com — Stockfish engine analysis (FENs only).
  • DigitalOcean — hosting.
  • Sentry / PostHog — error and product analytics.

Data deletion

Email {{ support_email }} to request deletion of your tenant record and all stored games. Cancellation through /billing stops all polling immediately and deletes data after 30 days.
