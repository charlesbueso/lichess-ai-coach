Frequently asked

Will Lichess ban me for using this?

No. We use Lichess's public games API with a polite User-Agent and stay well under their rate limits. Lichess explicitly allows this kind of read-only commercial use.

Is this cheating?

No. Chess Brain only analyzes completed games. It never assists during play, doesn't connect to your client, and never sees your live position. Use it the same way you'd use a coach reviewing your games afterward.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Run /billing in your server to open Stripe's customer portal — cancel, update card, or download invoices in two clicks. No retention emails, no friction.

What do you store about my games?

The minimum required to post analysis: your Lichess username, game IDs we've already coached (so we don't repeat ourselves), and the analysis text we publish to your channel. Full PGNs are pulled from Lichess on demand and not retained. See Privacy for specifics.

Does it track my stats or compare me to other players?

No. Chess Brain only knows about your games, inside your server. There are no leaderboards, no rating curves stored, no benchmarks against other subscribers. The whole point is a private conversation between you and the engine — no social pressure, no surveillance, just honest coaching.
